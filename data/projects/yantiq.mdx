## The problem

Early Arabic literacy can be difficult for preschool-aged children, especially those growing up in non-Arabic-speaking environments. Many learners do not get enough consistent exposure to correct Arabic pronunciation, and existing learning tools often rely on static content without meaningful real-time speaking feedback.

Most available solutions also lack a structured progression from **letter recognition → words → sentences**, making it harder to build reading skills step by step. Listening, speaking, and reading are often treated as separate activities instead of one connected learning experience.

Parents and teachers also have limited visibility into a child’s progress when platforms do not provide useful tracking, scoring, or adaptive support.

Yantiq was designed to address these gaps with an intelligent, interactive, and child-friendly Arabic learning experience focused on early reading and pronunciation.

## What I built

Yantiq is a mobile-first Arabic learning platform that combines structured literacy lessons, audio-first activities, gamification, and AI-powered pronunciation evaluation.

The MVP is organized around **five learning levels with multiple stages**, using short lessons that guide children from letters to words and eventually simple sentences. Lessons combine visual recognition with listening and speaking activities so children can hear a target, repeat it, and receive immediate feedback.

A core part of the system is a **fine-tuned Arabic speech model** adapted from an existing Quranic speech/recitation model to Modern Standard Arabic. During a speaking activity, the system:

1. Presents a target letter, word, or sentence.
2. Stores the expected answer as predefined ground truth.
3. Records the child’s spoken response.
4. Sends the audio to a separate AI service layer.
5. Transcribes the speech using the fine-tuned model.
6. Compares the transcription with the expected ground truth.
7. Generates a correctness result, score, and feedback indicators.
8. Returns guided, encouraging feedback to the child.
9. Stores the score for progress tracking.

The learning flow uses a **fixed path with light adaptation**. Children must meet pass criteria before unlocking the next stage. When they struggle, the system first provides hints and retry opportunities; repeated difficulty can trigger a simpler version of the task.

To keep the experience engaging, the MVP includes **stars, badges, and level unlocking**. More advanced features such as daily streaks, avatars, dynamic AI personalization, an AI voice companion, and a full parent/teacher dashboard are intentionally deferred to later phases.

### System architecture

Yantiq uses a modular architecture with four main components:

- **Mobile application layer** — handles child-facing lessons, audio playback, speaking interactions, rewards, onboarding, and progress views.
- **Main backend server** — manages guardian accounts, child profiles, lesson progression, scoring, rewards, and communication with the AI service.
- **AI service layer** — handles audio preprocessing, speech inference, transcription, ground-truth comparison, pronunciation evaluation, scoring, and feedback generation.
- **Relational database** — stores guardian accounts, child profiles, lesson structures, performance results, rewards, and progress history.

Security is applied across the system through guardian authentication, authorization, secure communication between services, input validation, child-data protection, and basic abuse prevention.

### Target users

The primary users are **children learning early Arabic literacy**, while **parents and teachers** act as secondary users who support onboarding and monitor progress.

### Success criteria

The project defines measurable targets for the MVP, including:

- Speech evaluation results returned for at least **90% of attempts**.
- At least **80% transcription accuracy** on test cases.
- At least **90% correct ground-truth comparison** on valid test cases.
- At least **80% correct pronunciation classification**.
- Average AI feedback latency of **2 seconds or less**.
- Backend API success rate of at least **95%**.
- At least **80% of child test sessions** completed without external help.
- Typical lesson completion time of **2–5 minutes**.

The overall goal is to deliver a complete, testable core product that demonstrates strong value in **Arabic literacy, speech AI, mobile learning, backend integration, progress tracking, and child-safe system design** without overloading the first release with high-risk advanced features.
