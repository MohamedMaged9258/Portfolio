## Problem

Concurrency is easy to describe and hard to feel. I wanted a project that makes **round-robin scheduling** and multithreading tangible rather than abstract.

## Approach

- Modeled each racing car as its own **thread**, advancing in turns under a round-robin scheme.
- Coordinated **eight concurrent threads**, managing shared state safely so the race stays fair and consistent.

## Outcome

Running the cars concurrently improved processing speed by roughly **300%** compared with a single-threaded approach — a concrete demonstration of what concurrency buys you, and of the coordination it demands.
